FAQ

Common questions.

Does the AI replace transport modellers?

No. The assistant takes on the repetitive parts of the work: translating a question into scenarios, preparing data, running and re-running models and drafting comparisons. Our modellers review the assumptions, validate the outputs and sign off the conclusions.

What data do you need from us?

It depends on the question. Typical inputs are traffic counts, road network data, signal plans and any existing models or planning documents. We can start from public and open data and add your data where it improves the result. The data audit in every report records what was used and what was assumed.

Which modelling tools do you use?

We combine established traffic simulation platforms with our own optimisation and machine-learning models, and we can work with the tools and formats your organisation already uses. Tell us what you have and we will tell you how we would approach it.

How is this different from asking a general AI chatbot?

A chatbot can describe a lane closure; it cannot run a calibrated traffic model. In our workflow the language model drafts scenarios and explanations, the numbers come from simulation, and a modeller checks both before anything reaches you.

How do we know the AI has not made something up?

You confirm the structured scenario specification before anything runs, every figure in the report comes from a model run that is kept and can be re-run, the assumptions and limitations are listed, and a modeller signs off the conclusions.

How long does a study take?

Scoping and data preparation set the pace; once a scenario is specified and the inputs are validated, running and comparing options is fast. We agree a timeframe at scoping and keep you informed as the work progresses.

How are results validated?

Baseline runs are checked against observed data before alternatives are compared. Modellers review calibration, plausibility and edge cases, and the report states the assumptions and the limits of the analysis.

Can we see and reuse the models?

Yes. Scenario files, configurations and outputs are available on request, so your team or your consultants can reproduce and build on the results.

How is our data handled?

We agree in writing what data will be used, how it will be stored and when it will be deleted before anything is shared, and we use it only for the agreed study. See our privacy statement.
