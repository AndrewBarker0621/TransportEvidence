Privacy

Privacy statement.

How Transport Evidence handles information about visitors to this website and people who contact us.

This website

This website is a static site. It does not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts, and it does not collect personal information from visitors. The site loads the Inter typeface from Google Fonts; when your browser requests those font files, Google receives your browser's request information, which is subject to Google's privacy policy.

Contacting us

If you email us, we keep your message and contact details so that we can reply and, if you decide to work with us, to deliver the work. We do not sell or share your contact details with third parties, and we do not add you to mailing lists without asking.

Project data

Data you share with us for a study is handled under a written agreement that sets out what will be used, how it will be stored, who can access it and when it will be deleted. We use project data only for the agreed study.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email admin@transportevidence.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Changes

We will update this statement if our practices change. This version was last updated in October 2026.
