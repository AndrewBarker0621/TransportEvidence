Product

An AI assistant for transport modelling, with modellers in the loop.

Transport Evidence turns a plain-language planning question into structured scenarios, validated inputs, simulation and optimisation runs, and a report that explains the evidence. People review every assumption and sign off every conclusion.

The workflow

From question to evidence in four stages.

1

Ask

You describe the question in plain language: "What happens to the corridor if we close the kerbside lane between 7 and 9 am for six weeks of roadworks?" The assistant asks clarifying questions where the request is ambiguous, for example which streets are in scope, which time periods matter and which outcomes you care about.

2

Scenario and data preparation

The request becomes a structured scenario specification: the network changes to apply, the demand assumptions to use and the performance metrics to evaluate. In parallel, the assistant prepares and checks the input data, reconciling traffic counts, road network data, signal plans and planning documents, and flags gaps and inconsistencies for a modeller to resolve.

3

Simulation and optimisation

The assistant orchestrates our modelling and optimisation tools: it runs the baseline, runs each alternative, and iterates towards better-performing options, for example adjusting signal offsets or the timing of a closure. Modellers check calibration and sanity at each step.

4

Comparison and explanation

Results are compared side by side and explained in plain language: what changed, by how much, where, and why. The report links every conclusion to the evidence behind it, and a modeller signs it off before it reaches you.

What you get

Deliverables you can act on, and audit.

Scenario specification

Your question translated into explicit network changes, demand assumptions and evaluation metrics, for your review before anything runs.

Validated inputs

A data audit that records the sources used, the gaps and inconsistencies found, and the assumptions made to fill them.

Baseline and alternatives

Our approach to AI

  • AI assists, people decide. A qualified modeller reviews every assumption and signs off every conclusion.
  • Numbers come from simulation, not from the language model. The assistant drafts scenario specifications and report text; travel times, delays and queues come from the simulation engine.
  • Transparent assumptions. Every report lists the network edits, demand assumptions and parameters behind each scenario.
  • Reproducible runs. Scenario files, configurations and outputs are kept so results can be re-run and audited.
  • Limits stated. Each report says what the analysis does and does not cover, and where the data was thin.

Illustrative example

A lane closure, three ways.

A worked example of the kind of question we answer. The scenario and figures are illustrative, not results from a real study.

Question "We need to close the kerbside lane on a two-lane arterial for six weeks. What is the least disruptive way to do it?"
Scenario AAll-day closure

Simplest for the contractor. Adds roughly two minutes of delay per vehicle in the AM peak and pushes traffic onto two parallel local streets.

Scenario BOff-peak closure only

Keeps corridor delay within about a minute of baseline across the day, at the cost of a longer works programme.

Scenario COff-peak closure with retimed signals

Retiming two downstream intersections recovers most of the remaining delay. Found by the optimisation step, not proposed in the brief.

Report Recommends Scenario C, documents the assumptions about demand and diversion, and flags the one count site where data was missing.

Modelling approach

Built on established methods.

We combine established traffic simulation platforms with our own optimisation and machine-learning models, and we work with the tools and data formats your organisation already uses. Metrics follow standard practice: travel time, delay, queue length, throughput, level of service and network-wide performance, by time period and location.

Your data

Handled by agreement, used only for your study.

Before any data is shared, we agree in writing what will be used, how it will be stored and when it will be deleted. Data is used only for the agreed study, and we can work within your organisation's data-sharing and procurement requirements. See our privacy statement.
