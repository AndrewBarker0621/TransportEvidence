About

Built by transport researchers.

Transport Evidence was founded in Sydney by two PhD researchers in transport engineering who work on AI, simulation and optimisation for urban mobility every day. Their research is published in IEEE Transactions on Cybernetics, Transportation Research Part C, Measurement and Sustainable Cities and Society.

Mission

More options, evaluated earlier.

Our goal is to make high-quality, evidence-based transport analysis faster and more accessible, so cities can evaluate more options before committing to costly infrastructure decisions.

Why we started

Modelling should not be the bottleneck.

In our research we build city-scale simulations and optimisation models every week. In practice, testing a single scenario still takes specialist modellers days of manual network building, configuration and output analysis. We started Transport Evidence to close that gap, without removing the expert judgement that makes the results trustworthy.

Why now

Three things have changed.

Language models can read the brief

Modern language models can turn a planning question and a stack of documents into a precise, structured scenario, and explain results in plain language. That removes the slowest manual steps.

Simulation and optimisation can run at scale

City-scale simulation and optimisation that once needed a lab now run on ordinary cloud infrastructure, so many options can be tested in the time one used to take.

We do both for a living

Our research is reinforcement learning, optimisation and simulation for urban mobility. We know where the models break and what a modeller needs to check.

Founders

The team.

Ruihao (Andrew) Zeng

Co-founder

Andrew is a PhD researcher in transportation at TransportLab, The University of Sydney, where he also completed an M.Phil. with High Distinction. His research covers distributed autonomous vehicles, deep reinforcement learning for multi-agent vehicle control, LiDAR-based 3D perception and multi-object tracking, and spatio-temporal traffic prediction. He builds end-to-end systems, from data pipelines and large-scale simulation to real-time cloud deployment, and has presented his work at the IFAC World Congress and the Transportation Research Board Annual Meeting.
