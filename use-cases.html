Use cases

Questions we help answer.

Each engagement starts with a question in plain language. Here is what we model for the most common ones, and what you receive at the end.

Lane closures and roadworks

"What happens if we close this lane during peak hours, and is there a less disruptive way to schedule the works?"

What we model
Capacity changes on the affected links, diversion onto parallel routes, queue spillback at upstream intersections, and alternative closure windows.
What you get
Delay and diversion impacts by time of day for each option, plus a recommended works schedule with the evidence behind it.

Signal timing changes

"Can we retime this corridor to cut delay without pushing the problem onto the side streets?"

What we model
Signal plans, offsets and phasing across the corridor or network, with optimisation searching for better-performing plans.
What you get
Candidate signal plans ranked by corridor and network performance, with side-street impacts made explicit.

New developments

"What is the traffic impact of a 400-dwelling development on this precinct, and which mitigation works are needed?"

What we model
Added trips by time period, access arrangements, intersection performance and the effect of candidate mitigation measures.
What you get
An impact assessment comparing mitigation options, ready to inform a traffic impact statement or a planning response.

Public transport changes

"If we reroute this bus and add priority at three intersections, what happens to buses, and to everyone else?"

What we model
Route and frequency changes, bus priority measures and their interaction with general traffic across the wider network.
What you get
Bus travel time and reliability alongside general traffic impacts, for each option.

Network optimisation

"Across all the small changes we could make in this area, which combination performs best?"

What we model
Many candidate interventions evaluated and combined by the optimisation step, against the metrics you choose.
What you get
A shortlist of the best-performing combinations with the trade-offs between them.

Policy and strategy questions

"What does this policy document imply for our network, and how would we test it?"

What we model
Policy intent translated into testable scenarios with transparent assumptions, from parking changes to speed limits to demand management.
What you get
Scenario definitions you can review, results for each, and a plain-language summary for non-technical readers.

Who we build for

Councils, state agencies and consultancies.

For councils

Independently check the traffic impact assessments submitted with development applications, test local area traffic management and parking changes, and give councillors quantified options rather than opinions.

For state agencies

Generate and compare many more options early in business-case development, test corridor and network interventions consistently, and keep a transparent assumptions trail for assurance reviews.

For consultancies

Add scenario-testing capacity on tight programmes without adding headcount. We work alongside your team and use your models where they exist.
